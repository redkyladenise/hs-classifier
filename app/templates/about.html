About the HS Classifier

This system helps customs brokers and small importers find the right HS code for their products using Philippine import data, up to the 8-digit level. HS/AHTN is crucial for trade compliance. This serves as an assistive tool, not an official classification.

Smart Suggestions

Suggests the best matching codes from 2-digit (Chapter), 4-digit (Heading), 6-digit (Subheading), to 8-digit (AHTN) based on your product description.

Confidence & Retrieval

Shows how sure the model is at every level with calibrated confidence, and retrieves similar past declarations to help you decide.

Why a Hierarchy of Small Models?

Customs descriptions are messy. Brokers often use vague terms, incomplete descriptions, or brand names, making exact HS code prediction difficult.

A hierarchical model breaks this problem into smaller steps using logistic regression. Instead of predicting an 8-digit code in one leap, it progressively narrows down the possibilities while keeping the top 5 most promising paths at each level.

How it works

  • 1. Clean and normalize the product description.
  • 2. Predict HS codes from 2 to 8 digits.
  • 3. Provide confidence scores for predictions.
  • 4. Retrieve similar past declarations as references.

Covered HS Chapters

This tool focuses on five selected Harmonized System chapters based on historical Philippine customs declaration records: Chapters 39 (Plastics), 73 (Iron and Steel), 84 (Machinery), 85 (Electrical Equipment), and 87 (Vehicles and Parts).

Chapter 39: Plastics and Articles Thereof
Chapter 73: Articles of Iron or Steel
Chapter 84: Nuclear Reactors, Boilers, Machinery and Mechanical Appliances; Parts Thereof
Chapter 85: Electrical Machinery and Equipment; Parts and Accessories Thereof
Chapter 87: Vehicles Other Than Railway or Tramway Rolling-Stock; Parts and Accessories Thereof

Data & Disclaimer

This system provides classification guidance using public historical Philippine import data. Please note the following:

Trained on public historical Philippine import data and AHTN schedules, covering five selected chapters.
Aligned with the AHTN 2022 nomenclature implemented in the Philippines.
User descriptions are not stored, and retrieved historical descriptions are anonymized.
Generic descriptions (e.g., "Parts" or "Accessories") may produce low-confidence predictions.
Confidence scores indicate model certainty, not guaranteed accuracy or legal correctness.
For official classifications, consult the Philippine Tariff Finder or the AHTN 2022 Tariff Book.
